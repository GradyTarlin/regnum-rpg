⬡ The Galaxy of Regnum

Regnum is a galaxy of civilizations that never stands still. Empires rise and crumble, rebellions ignite across the stars, and impossible technologies reshape what it means to be alive. The Animator is free to set any game in Regnum or in another sci-fi setting of their choosing — the systems here are designed to flex to your table.

Seven major powers divide the known galaxy: the corporate hegemony of Apex Corporation, the idealistic federalism of Magnus-V, the spiritual paradise of Voidreach Unity and its lawless Outer Ring, the industrial republic of the Banderog, the ferocious Chkaari Empire, and the free-spirited racing circuit of King K. Every faction is a living ecosystem of societies, technologies, crimes, and mysteries — and every player character finds their place within them.
