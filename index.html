What is Regnum RPG?

Adventure Across a Living Galaxy

Regnum is a streamlined, story-first TTRPG designed for fast sessions and meaningful choices. Every decision ripples through a galaxy bristling with factions, secrets, and opportunity.

🧑‍🚀

Build Your Crew

Choose from 9 distinct species and 7 powerful classes, each with unique skill bonuses that define your role in the crew. No two characters play the same way.

🎲

Roll the Dice

All skill checks use a clean 2d6 system — roll, add your skill bonus, and beat the target. High stakes, high drama. The numbers are small; the moments are enormous.

✨

Shape the Story

The Animator — Regnum's term for the Game Master — uses intuitive, open-ended tools to build living sessions. Factions shift, loyalties fracture, and your choices echo across the stars.

The Galaxy of Regnum

Seven Powers Shape the Stars

Competing ideologies, ancient grudges, and uneasy alliances define a galaxy in constant flux. Where does your loyalty lie?

Apex Corporation

Sleek corporate power with holographic technology and chemical VR. The galaxy's dominant corporate force.

Magnus-V Federation

Eco-brutalist harmony between people, robots and nature, all watched by the cybernetic ruler Gagarin.

Voidreach Unity

A regal tropical utopia of rich purples, lush foliage, gold, and gleaming ecological paradise.

Voidreach Outer Ring

An impoverished, arid dystopia of rust and scarcity, the shadow beneath the Unity's gleam.

Banderog Republic

An industrial powerhouse fueled by chemicals, transparent tubes, and heavy smog-belching machinery.

Chkaari Empire

An infernal war-hive lit by green Stormfire. Jagged architecture, blood culture, and legions of warriors.

King K's Circuit

A lawless haven of racers, pirates, and adventurers led by the rebel hero King K. Casinos and freedom reign.
